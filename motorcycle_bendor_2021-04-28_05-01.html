Motorcycle Trip to Bend, Oregon. April 28, 2021 Thru May 01, 2021.


A map of the route to Bend Oregon and ride the roads around Bend. Since it was the end of April a few of the roads at the higher evelvations that we wanted to ride were closed. Lesson learned, plan the roads you want to go on and check the road status.



The four-day trip

  • Trip Day One April 28, 2021: Ride south headed to Bend Oregon, 390+ mile ride. The ride was amazing all day long - Click for details of Day 1 of the ride ...


  • Trip Day Two April 29, 2021: Ride around Bend Oregon. TRIED to get to Newberry Volcano Craters, but the road was more offroad than onroad, Tried the McKenzie Highway pass, it was closed - Click for details of Day 2 of the ride ...


  • Trip Day Three April 30, 2021: Tried the west entrance to Newberry Volcano Craters, also closed. Ah well,beautiful day to ride - Click for details of Day 3 of the ride ...


  • Trip Day Four May 1, 2021: Ride from Bend, OR back. The ride was sunshine and great weather all day long with some twisty roads thrown in for good measure - Click for details of Day 4 of the ride ...
