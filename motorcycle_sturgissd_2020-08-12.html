Motorcycle Trip from Idaho Falls, Idaho to Baker City, Oregon via Craters of the Moon - August 12, 2020


A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from August 4 thru August 13th.

Left Idaho Falls and went to Craters of the Moon. Lava everywhere. Just a reminder that this area of the country is still geologically active.


Driving around the Craters of the Moon is amazing. To think that this volcanic activity in the middle of the United States is only a couple of thousand years old is amazing, geologically just yesterday.

Everywhere you see the plants JUST STARTING to come back and poke through the rock


The landscape really DOES look like you are on the moon:


On down the road we rode along a straight road until we got to Highway 20. Finally. Some curves.

From there we went to Baker City, Oregon to our lodgings for the night.
