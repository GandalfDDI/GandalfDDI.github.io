June 13 - From Hoh Rainforest back Home



Rode from the Hoh campground out to 101 and then on to the Kingston-Edmonds Ferry. Again, jumped to the head of the line since I am on a motorcycle. This is the Slow TV Hoh Rainforest Back Home 20260613 ride.

Nice thing about taking a ferry on the motorcycle is I can bypass all the car lining up to get on the vessel. Washington State Ferries loads motorcycles first as an operational convenience rather than a legal entitlement - bikes tuck into the bow, against bulkheads, and into the odd gaps a car can't use, so staging them up front maximizes deck utilization and gets them off the boat fastest on the other end. It's discretionary, though: under WAC 468-300-700 motorcycles aren't a designated preferential-loading category, so the deck crew extends the courtesy because it helps their staging, not because they owe it to you - arrive after loading has started and they'll put you on last, on the back among the cars.
