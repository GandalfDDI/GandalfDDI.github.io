Motorcycle Trip to Stites, Idaho August 4, 2020


A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from August 4 thru August 13th.

The obligatory start of ride picture, all ready to go:


We met in I-90 and started our ride going across Snoqualmie Pass. From there just to keep it a little more scenic we rode the Palouse Scenic Parkway, Route 26. Othello is a good stopping point for food and gas.

We were still settling in for the ride, didn't take much in the way of pictures. Crossed the eastern portion of Washington with nice flowing fields of grass.

Getting near Lewiston we stopped at the outlook over the City. Fantastic view of the valley, a nice picture of Cody and his wife Ryan overlooking the valley:


We stopped at Gibbs Eddy Access Area to make some equipment adjustments, nice view




On the other side of Lewiston you drop down, a video as we rode along State Route 12 and the Clearwater River, amazing sky and road.

From there we found our lodgings for the night.
