Southwest National Parks Motorcycle Trip, Days 5 to 8. August 17 – 20, 2026.



Trip Day Five – Monday, August 17, 2026: Pinnacles to Sequoia National Park (~190 miles)

It was a short ride from Pinnacles to Sequoia. Good twisties going into the park. Unfortunately the camera cut out on the ride to Sequoia so no video of twisties.

The General Sherman tree, one of the 'must see' trees because it is such a large Sequoia, these trees are so tall getting it in one frame is difficult. The General Sherman Tree in the Giant Forest, Sequoia is the largest single-trunk tree on Earth by volume that has been found so far:


Jo in front of the General Sherman tree for reference / size comparison, yes the tree is huge:


A grove of Sequoia. Because they are so big I had to take multiple shots and used Image Composite Editor (ICE) to paste them together. You can see some of the burn scars from past forest fires on these trees, another reason to see these parks before climate change makes it all sad:
