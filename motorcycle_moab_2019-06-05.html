Motorcycle Trip From Richfield, UT, Visit Capitol Reef National Park then on to Moab 2019-06-05


A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from May 30 thru June 14th.

We stayed in Richfield, UT and travelled back to Capitol Reef National Park. We went to the Capitol Reef Scenic drive (here is a shorter clip of the drive taken from the pillion seat) and we stopped at Eph Hanks Tower. As you can guess high towering walls:



Another nice sunshine day for a ride to Moab. Another short short clip of the drive to Moab taken from the pillion seat showing the vast landscape and green farms.

As you can see from the previous clip and this video of the ride the landscape progressed from lots of farms to sand dunes down Utah Highway 24 (Past the Swing Arm City OHV area, 8:11 in the video). As you have more and more bluffs you MUST be cognizant of the wind. When you go around the end of a cliff or bluff be ready for the wind to shift in either directlion. At one stop the gas station owner told us a rider had been blow off his motorcycle as he rounded a corner by the wind.

From there we went on to Moab to our hotel.
