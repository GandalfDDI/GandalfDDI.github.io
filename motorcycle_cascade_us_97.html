Motorcycle Trip Through The Cascades On United States 97 - Blewett Pass Washington

As with all the cascade passes, Blewett Pass / US 97 offers views of the mountains, deep forest and lots of twisties.

Starting from Leavenworth Washington, a town known for its Bavarian style architecture and themes:
