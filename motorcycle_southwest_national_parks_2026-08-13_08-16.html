Trip Day Three – Saturday, August 15, 2026: Red Bluff, California to Pinnacles National Park (292 miles)

That rather large group of riders left ahead of me the next morning. More power to them for riding together but I was reminded why I like smaller groups. A few miles down the road they were all pulled off to the side of the road. One rider has a problem everybody stops. It is also more load for the lead rider to keep track of everybody. Too much for me personally. One or two other people riding along is most comfortable for me.
Arrived at Pinnacles National Park, set up camp, and caught the deer wandering through the campground – a common theme on these trips. My guess was that the deer feel safer around campsites because the predators keep their distance from people. Turns out researchers have studied this and call it the 'Human Shield Hypothesis' (though some studies find only limited support for it):
