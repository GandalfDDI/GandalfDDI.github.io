Trip Day Thirty – Friday, September 11, 2026: Safford to Show Low, Arizona on the Coronado Trail (213 miles)

This ride was an extra bonus. I left Safford (headed east for a while, then you veer left taking highway 191 north. About 45 minutes after leaving Safford I arrived at the Morenci open-pit copper and molybdenum mining complex, owned by Freeport-McMoRan. Coronado Trail Hwy 191 goes right through that working mine. Large trucks on the road. And above you. And beside you. 10 foot tires. From the Morenci city limit which sits about 3,500 ft, the Coronado Trail climbs past the open-pit mine and up through high desert into fir and aspen forest near 9,000 ft. That 92-mile stretch to Alpine packs in more than 400 curves, tight twisties all the way up to Hannagan Meadow and then the road relaxes into easy sweepers for the last miles into Alpine. This is purported to be the twistiest road in the United States in that short of a distance.

Specifically:
  • The roads inside the open pit mine LITERALLY changes under your feet as the mining company wishes and my Garmin hadn't kept up. The GPS directed me into a fence
  • Curve counts vary by source: 460 curves between Clifton and Springerville (ADOT), 435 counted between the Morenci city limit and Alpine (RoadTrip America), and about 427 between Clifton and Alpine (motorcycleroads.com)
  • No official count of 10 or 15 MPH curves exists but MOST of the curves are less than 25 MPH corners, you will not be going the posted speed limit
  • No shoulders, steep drop-offs, and storm debris washed across the road at times – ride it at the posted speeds (or less)
  • The road gets noticibly less cared for once you get past the mine
