Motorcycle ride along the Columbia River from Fruitland, WA to Revelstoke Canada 2022-09-06




The Invisibilia podcast discussed Norwegian 'Slow TV'. I decided to make this entire trip all Slow TV videos. This video is the ride for the entire day.

I left early in the morning headed north for the Canadian border. The ArriveCAN app is AMAZING. I strongly suggest that you go through the process to get logged in, registered BEFORE you cross the border.

I always look for nice places to stop, here is a view of Columbia River, with a classic shot my motorcycle. Starting to see trees and more greenery:


Continuing to head north I arrived at the Boundary Border. I stopped at the border to take more pictures of the Columbia River:


Welcome to British Columbia! :-) (and BTW, the region is pronounced 'coot-knee'):
