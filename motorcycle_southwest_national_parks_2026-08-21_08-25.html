Southwest National Parks Motorcycle Trip, Days 9 to 13. August 21 – 25, 2026.



Trip Day Nine – Friday, August 21, 2026: Pahrump, Nevada to Las Vegas, through Zion to Bryce Canyon, Utah (337 miles)

We came out of Pahrump making our way to Zion. Sad day :-( ... Jo's bike had a mechanical issue just past Las Vegas. As in all motorcycle adventures that is her story to tell. We had a discussion, there was a Triumph dealer a few miles back so she decided to head that way to get her bike fixed ... She made it back to the dealership but of course it wasn't that easy. Again, all her story to tell.

Trip Day Ten – Saturday, August 22, 2026: Bryce Canyon to Torrey, Utah on Highway 12 (147 miles)

While researching the trip I saw that sunrise was worth getting up for. It was:




I rode down to Rainbow Point, the end of the park. Lots of good roads and viewpoints. Got out early enough the road wasn't busy. Looking northeast from Rainbow Point across the Paria Valley to the Table Cliff Plateau, the same pink rock as the Bryce rim, lifted 1,000+ feet higher by the Paunsaugunt Fault:


And Ponderosa Canyon, Bryce Canyon – hoodoos at the rim, a ponderosa pine forest on the canyon floor 800 feet below:


Further on down the road headed towards Torrey Utah was The Blues and Powell Point from the Blues Overlook on UT-12: 80-million-year-old seabed shale below, the same pink rock as Bryce rising to 10,188 feet above:


After The Blues and Powell Point was the start of Grand Staircase-Escalante National Monument. As always LOTS of tourists driving slow.

Got to the hotel in Torrey Utah JUST as it was starting to rain. Within a short time it was literally a monsoon. Hail coming down. Weather warnings about flash floods, not the only time I ran into this on the trip:


Trip Day Eleven – Sunday, August 23, 2026: Torrey, Utah through Capitol Reef to Arches National Park (185 miles)

The next ride was from Torry with hopefully a canyon ride in Capitol Reef, but alas the monsoon from the night before closed the road:


Funny mototrcycle story on the road. Stopped for gas, guy and his wife next to me with riding gear on. 'Where you going?' - 'Arches' 'Really, mee too. Lets ride along'. I had a 'scenic' road picked ouit from looking at the maps. Suggested we try that road then go on to Arches. Losng story short ... Nope. Dirt road not worth taking a chance on, but while we were talking he suggested 'Potash Roah', he was going to ride that. Had a few conversations with other riders while getting gas on the road. Rarely ever happens when I am driving a car.
