Girl: No it's not. Please it's too scary!
Guy: Then tell me you love me.
Girl: Fine I love you. Slow down!
Guy: Now give me a BIG hug.
Girl hugs him

The REAL story ... The guy (John) came home from college because his parents needed him at home. Both of his parents had fallen deep into their Alcohol and Opioid addiction. The family farm, that had been in the family for 100 years, was about to go under and he was the only child left. John had spent his entire life trying to get out of this one-horse town, had managed by working 2 jobs to get a motorcycle and save enough to go to college. It was his only chance to get out. When he came back, he connected with old friends. One night a friend invited John to a party, Maisie was someone he remembered from school, a few grades younger. He had overheard her saying that she was perfectly happy staying right where she was in this town. They talked, had fun, got drunk. John woke up next to Maisie in bed, she looked at him, said "I always liked you in school and wanted you to be my boyfriend, guess that happened". John was somewhat surprised she already considered them to be boyfriend / girlfriend but dismissed it as her teasing him.

Maisie called him off and on at home but he ignored her. She was nice enough but he knew she would never leave this town, a town he desperately wanted to get out of. A few weeks later Maisie left a cryptic message on the phone "we REALLY need to talk". John rode over to her apartment above the one restaurant in town where she washed dishes. There she told him that she was pretty sure that she was going to have his child. He asked her if she would go on a ride with him on his motorcycle. She jumped on the back and they took off.

Johns world was closing in on him. The escape routes out of this town were disappearing and he had no way to get out. He had a large life insurance policy that would take care of his parents. As John got close to the bike, before he got on the bike he pulled off the hydraulic tube connected to the front brakes. On a road outside of town he started speeding up. Maisie asked him to slow down because she was scared. He asked her to let him know if she loved him - if she said yes (after talking to him for only one night) then he knew his life was doomed to being trapped. She emphatically said she loved him and would forever. Even thought he knew he had this one chance to kill himself, it would not be fair to take Maisie with him, he handed her his helmet and slammed into the building KNOWING THAT HE HAD A REAR BRAKE AND 4 LOWER GEARS TO SLOW HIS BIKE TO A STOP SAFELY!!!!!! (Yes, the first story is BS, all Motorcycles have 2 brakes, front and rear, and even if they fail downshifting will allow you to coast to a dead stop. Not "The brakes failed". Taking your helmet off and handing it to someone riding behind you, while not impossible, is difficult as you have to take both hands off the steering, while balancing the bike, release the chin strap, while pivoting to hand the helmet back to them and THEY have to know how to put it on. Learn about a subject before writing some silly story about how love, romance and death occur)
