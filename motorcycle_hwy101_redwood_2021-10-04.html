Motorcycle Ride From Agate Beach OR to Crescent City CA October 04, 2021


A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from October 2 thru October 8, 2021.

































Continued riding down US Highway 101, intermittant towns, fog and sunshine and wind. Be ready for the wind from the sea. In and out of the forests and LOTS of scenic pullouts. The first we stopped at was the Cape Perpetua Scenic Area Viewpoint, nice view high above looking out at the ocean:



And one looking down at the Cape Cove beach (there is a short trail down if you want to hike it):


Leaving Cape Cove going back south on US Highway 101 sunshine and fog. Started out sunshine, from this video but went in and out of fog. VERY nice ride, lots of pullouts, lots of twisties, the lens fogged up but you still get a feel for the road.

The second stop was at was the Pistol River North scenic area Viewpoint, nice walk onto the beach and looking down the shore:


Looking out at the haystack rocks in the water and another interesting wood artifact:


Further on south nearer to Pistol River North and past it on US Highway 101 STILL sunshine and fog. This video shows a little more of the road, after this it was pretty much fog for the rest of the ride. Couldn't see the ocean but all in all still good road. t

From there we went into Crescent City, CA for the night.
