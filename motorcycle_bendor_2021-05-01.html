Motorcycle From Bend, Oregon Back - May 1, 2021


A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from April 28 thru May 01, 2021.



Got up and out the door early for a long ride back home. Of course it was raining, luckily the only rain we had on the entire trip and it lasted a little over an hour. Where Highway 197 and 97 split I went left and she went right in the car. I had twisty roads to run :-).

After about 35 miles I turned right onto Sherars Bridge Hwy, Highway 216. I was headed for the twisty roads as they were calling. Nice roads, sharp drop-offs and LOTS of bicyclists.

Made my way back to Highway 97 and stopped for gas just past the Columbia river, always peaceful:


On the way back if I get a chance I always like to run Canyon Road always a scenic ride. Nice river ride with lots more twisties in this video. From there it was the Snoqualmie pass / Highway I-90 back.
