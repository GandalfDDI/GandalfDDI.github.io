Southwest National Parks Motorcycle Trip, Days 41 to 44. September 22 – 25, 2026.



Trip Day Forty-one – Tuesday, September 22, 2026: Corte Madera, California to Sue-meg State Park, California through the redwoods (288 miles)

I always enjoy riding up highway 101, in and out of scenic viewpoints of the Pacific ocean. The best part of the day's ride was the ride on 'The Avenue of the Giants', riding through the redwood forests. Coast redwoods (Sequoia sempervirens) are the tall ones in the fog and giant sequoias (Sequoiadendron giganteum) are the fat ones in the mountains. Sequoia, Kings Canyon and Yosemite have giant sequoias. Avenue of the Giants, Redwood National Park and Muir Woods have coast redwoods:

Trip Day Forty-two – Wednesday, September 23, 2026: Sue-meg State Park, California to Sunset Bay State Park, Oregon (190 miles)

A nice lady and her son stopped by on their bikes and we talked about the different parks and the journey I was on and what parks she had been to and her plans were for going to more parks. She took a picture for me at campsite:


Sunset Bay State Park was, like Sue-meg, buried in the trees. Peaceful ... mostly. The couple next to me decided to play music, loudly, I have a wide range of music I like so it didn't annoy me, but the songs sounded like they were AI generated, generic country. I could hear the words and they were different per song, but the beat and music sounded the same for every track. Someone from a couple of campsites over screamed loudly. Then THEY played THEIR music. Very Loudly. The couple next to me eventually got the message and turned down their music. All handled peaceably, if not a tad bit passive aggresive, but handled none the less. It was a very rare occurance for any of my camping experience. Usually it is quiet.

Trip Day Forty-three – Thursday, September 24, 2026: Sunset Bay, Oregon over the Astoria-Megler Bridge to Long Beach, Washington (260 miles)

View of Sunset Bay:


More riding up highway 101, in and out of scenic viewpoints of the Pacific ocean. At this point I was ready to get back home. And of course Washington State welcomed me with rain. No surprise there.

Trip Day Forty-four – Friday, September 25, 2026: Long Beach, Washington to home (189 miles)

The first day of mostly rain on my trip. In Washington State, of course. <sigh> Oh well, not unexpected. Washington welcomed me home and reminded me what it is to live here. Very on brand: no welcome mat, just a reminder that the moss doesn't grow itself. I left as early as I could in the first light to try to stay ahead of the Gale Warning of 30 to 40 knots. There were branches down as you can see in the video. EVENTUALLY I got to the other side, completely soaked. Thank goodness for heated seats and grips. I got home:
