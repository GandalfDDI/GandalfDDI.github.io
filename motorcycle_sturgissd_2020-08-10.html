A playlist of all of the videos from the ride from August 4 thru August 13th.

Home again home again Jig Jigitty Jig ... But not directly ;-) ... We had a somewhat circuitous route to get home. Ryan and Cody wanted to visit Yellowstone and I am always up for a visit there. I like that park.

BUT FIRST ... The ever looming and classic Devils Tower, shown in all the brochures to Sturgis. A column of igneous rock that may have appeared when the softer sedimentary stone around it eroded away. No matter how you look at it it is amazing and massive.
