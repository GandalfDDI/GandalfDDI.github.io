At the top of Lolo Pass right at the Idaho / Montana border is the Lolo Pass Visitor Center. Time to stop, take a break and shop for a pin for my coat. And take more pictures. Ryan waving to us all geared up:


Many hours later after lunch we rode through MacDonald Pass near Helena. Vast Panorama, Ryan and Cody off to the side:


The HD Ride Planner decided that we had enough nice roads and decided to route us to Route 287. We passed through Toston, MT and we went offroading as you can see in this video. Us on our heavy road bikes on dirt and loose gravel. Pick a track that has the least amount of gravel, keep off the brake, smooth shifting and smooth acceleration / deceleration. Just part of the experience.

And finally out to the wide flat high plains (4,000 / 5,000 feet elevation) of the wide wide west close to the Missouri Headwaters / Three Forks:


From there we headed to Belgrade for our lodgings for the night.
